# Room Proposal Studio: Pilot Plan

This is a proposed validation plan, not a record of interviews or sales.

## Customer And Offer

Start with independent interior designers and studios of one to five people
working on living-room refreshes in one Indian city. The offer is a shorter path
from a client's room photo to a reviewed concept and a supplier-backed estimate.
The core result is an agreed image and budget, with a record of revisions.

## Customer Interviews

Interview 10 designers. Ask them to walk through their most recent real proposal,
including how many tools they used, when they requested quotes, where feedback
arrived, how long revisions took, and how an approval was recorded. Ask for
permission before retaining any client imagery or project documents.

Use these prompts:

- Show the last living-room proposal you sent. What happened after you sent it?
- Which part took longest: concepts, costing, sourcing, or revisions?
- How did you know which image and budget the client had approved?
- What did you have to redo when the client changed an item?
- Which tools do you currently pay for, and what work would this replace?
- Would you use this on a current project? What would stop you?

Record observed workflow and actual behavior separately from stated interest.
Do not treat positive reactions to the demo as proof of demand.

## Five-Project Trial

Recruit five designers for one real project each. Let each designer enter actual
supplier prices; do not present model-generated prices as verified quotes.
Capture baseline preparation time from a comparable prior project. Observe use
of photo upload, concept revisions, quote entry, review links, and approval.
Follow up after the first proposal and after the client's decision.

Track:

| Measure | Definition |
| --- | --- |
| Activation | First real project with a concept and at least one budget item |
| Time to proposal | Minutes of active work before the first shared proposal |
| Revision rounds | New proposals required before a decision |
| Repeat use | A second real project created within 30 days |
| Decision completion | Client approval or explicit change request received |
| Generation cost | All provider attempts, including rejected outputs |
| Willingness to pay | Actual paid trial accepted, not survey intent |

Proposed gates: at least four of five pilots send a real proposal; three create a
second project; three accept a paid continuation; median proposal preparation time
improves by at least 30% against their stated baseline. These are experiment
targets, not benchmarks or existing product results. Revisit the scope if the
pilot fails these gates.

## Pricing Experiment

Test INR 1,499 and INR 2,999 per studio per month with explicit generation limits.
Offer a clear paid pilot and collect payment only through a configured payment
provider after commercial terms are prepared. Track render attempts, storage,
support time, payment fees, and gross margin. No unlimited rendering promise.

## Further Releases

After repeat usage is established, add a supplier catalog with permission to use
product imagery, dated price/availability information, and quote confirmation.
Begin with a small curated catalog from local suppliers rather than invented
inventory. Track whether customers actually request quotes or buy selected items.

Treat continuous video redesign as a separate feasibility study. Benchmark room
geometry, temporal consistency, occlusion, product identity, latency, and total
cost per usable session across real devices before offering it commercially.
